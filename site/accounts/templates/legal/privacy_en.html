1. Who we are

Belcentra provides an online portal to condominium syndicates (“condos”). This policy explains what personal information we hold, why, who can see it and the rights you have, in line with Québec’s Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector and Canada’s federal privacy law.

2. Person in charge of personal information

Belcentra is responsible for protecting the personal information it holds. To reach the person in charge of the protection of personal information, write to support@belcentra.com.

3. What we collect

  • Your account: name, email address, preferred language, the condos you belong to and your role (owner, tenant, board member). Your password is stored only in a form that can’t be read back; if you use two-step sign-in, we keep the key that checks your codes.
  • Information your condo keeps about you: unit number, phone number, contact details, fees and payments, requests and repairs, documents, votes and messages, as entered by the board or by you.
  • Security records: dates of sign-ins and important account changes, with the IP address used, to protect your account and investigate abuse.

We don’t collect information for advertising and we don’t sell or rent personal information.

4. Why we use it

  • to let you sign in and open the condos you have access to;
  • to provide the portal’s features to your condo (accounting, requests, documents, votes, announcements);
  • to keep accounts secure and prevent fraud or abuse;
  • to answer your questions and support requests;
  • to meet our legal obligations.

5. Who can see it

  • Your condo’s board and the people it authorizes, according to their role in the portal.
  • Other residents of your condo see only what the portal shows them, for example the directory if your condo uses it, or a request you made public.
  • Belcentra staff, only when needed to run, secure or support the service.
  • Our service providers that host the portal and its data, under agreements that protect your information. We don’t share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

6. Where it is kept

Our main database is hosted in Canada, in the Montréal region. Some service providers we rely on (for example for hosting and delivering the website) may process limited information outside Québec. Before doing so, we assess the risks and make sure the information receives adequate protection.

7. Cookies

Belcentra uses only the cookies needed to keep you signed in, protect forms, remember a device you chose to save and remember your language. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your account while you have access to a condo. Information your condo keeps (for example payments and records the syndicate must retain under the law) is kept as long as your condo uses Belcentra and as required by its obligations. When your condo stops using Belcentra, its data is returned or deleted as agreed with the condo, and in any case deleted or anonymized within a reasonable time.

9. Security

We protect information with encrypted connections, hashed passwords, two-step sign-in for people who manage a building, separate data for each condo and limited access. If a confidentiality incident presents a risk of serious injury, we will notify the people concerned and the Commission d’accès à l’information as the law requires.

10. Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to receive it in a structured format, or to withdraw your consent where it applies. Some requests are handled with your condo’s board, which decides about the information it manages. Write to support@belcentra.com; we answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may file a complaint with the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec.

11. Changes

When we update this policy, we will ask you to accept the new version the next time you sign in. The date at the top shows the current version.
